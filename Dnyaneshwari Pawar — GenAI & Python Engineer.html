01 · About

Engineer first, researcher by habit.

I focus on the part of GenAI that is hard: making answers grounded, verifiable and safe to ship. That means retrieval with source citations, abstaining when the evidence isn’t there, deterministic tools for anything numeric, and APIs that hold up under real users.

My work spans RAG, LangGraph agents, Gemini / Groq LLMs, FastAPI, PostgreSQL + pgvector and React. Alongside, I co-authored TA-RAG, a paper on statement-level confidence calibration to cut hallucination in regulatory knowledge systems.

I’ve also worked with an industry partner: a dairy cooperative where I shipped scheduling automation and a festival-aware demand forecaster, so I’m used to messy data and real stakeholders.
